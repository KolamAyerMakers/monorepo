# Code of Conduct

Contributors must be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to [pmuller+github@kolamayermakers.org](mailto:pmuller+github@kolamayermakers.org). Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect participants and the project.
