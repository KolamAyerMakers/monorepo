# At the Prompt: purchased anthology

- This directory is for assembling the five purchased Julia Evans zines, not
  creating an original illustrated book. Track pending work in `TODO.md`.
- Preserve complete original pages, reading order, credits, proportions, vector
  artwork and text. Keep the approved covers and foreword editable.
- All documentation and book additions are English.
- Purchased PDFs, transcripts and derived PDFs/previews remain private and
  Git-ignored under `local/`; external source files stay in their original location.
  Never publish them or upload them as CI artifacts. CI uses only source-free tests.
- Do not contact publishers/printers or transmit files without explicit permission.
- Exports remain provisional until printer specifications and reading size are
  approved. Do not imply that a physical proof has been approved.
