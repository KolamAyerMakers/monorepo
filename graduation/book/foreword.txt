I started this course because I wanted to volunteer and contribute something practical to our community. Hands-on skills take practice, and I hoped I could help make that first step easier. Thank you for your trust and the time you put into learning. I've loved this experience, and I hope you have too.

One moment that will stay with me is our troubleshooting session. The AI tutor broke things in your environments; you investigated. You read logs, tried commands, interpreted error messages, and fixed problems. Seeing some of you discover the pleasure of figuring things out was a highlight for me. That curiosity is what helps you learn independently and become really good at what you do.

I hope you'll make Linux and the command line part of your everyday toolkit. Today's AI tools have learned from vast amounts of open-source code and documentation. Linux and command-line tools are a natural fit for them. Knowing how to use these tools yourself gives you a strong foundation for directing AI agents and building things with them.

This course has inspired me to create more opportunities for us to learn and build together. For me, this is a beginning. I hope it is for you too. And I hope we'll meet again soon.
