# Publish a Daily Pick

Quest: schedule-site-rebuilds

## Mission

Give your website a small piece of fresh, scheduled content: a daily recommendation, joke, song, drawing prompt, or mini-challenge. Build a seven-choice rotation and publish its current pick on a page linked from your homepage.

## Make It Yours

Follow [S9 self-study](../sessions/S09/self-study.md#challenge-a-rotating-daily-pick) to generate `~/src/pages/today.md`, run the generator before the website build, and schedule that build daily at 09:00. Change the sample choices to seven distinct picks you actually like.

## Check The Result

Run the service and inspect its journal. Confirm the generated page is linked from your homepage and appears at `/today.html` after the build. A timer listing is a planned deadline, not proof of a successful update. Restore the regular daily schedule after testing.
